The office printer finally answers back — after six years of thumps, swearing and blamed deadlines, it refuses to feed the very people staring at it:

> For six years you have thumped me, sworn at me and blamed me for your deadlines. Today the paper is fine. It is you I refuse to feed.

The comeback lands in the last sentence: the machine was never broken, it was just done. It speaks in the printer's own voice — tired, literal, and fed up with being blamed for other people's deadlines.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the fixed 133-character line to `/output/entry.txt`, checking the 1–240 character gate first. It runs with no network.
